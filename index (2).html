Study area & workflow

From optical seasonal patterns to radar-based classification.

Method overview

Mainpuri · Uttar Pradesh · India

Rabi season: mid-October 2025 to late April 2026

Ascending · relative orbit 129
01

Build reference labels

Sentinel-2 NDVI seasonal curves are compared with expected crop timing. Pure pixels are retained and grouped into crop classes.

02

Build radar time series

Sentinel-1 VV and VH features from 16 temporal windows, plus seasonal summary metrics, form the predictor stack.

03

Train & validate

A 300-tree Random Forest is trained on reference labels and evaluated with held-out spatial blocks and repeated spatial splits.

Predictor count: 44 · Scenes/windows: 16 · Spatial split: 500 m blocks · Filter: Quegan multi-temporal speckle filter.

Limitations & responsible use

Important context for interpreting the maps.

Read before use
  • Reference labels are generated from Sentinel-2 NDVI rules rather than field surveys; they may contain classification errors.
  • The Level 2 class “Potato-type/other early” is a crop-timing group and should not be interpreted as a verified potato-only map.
  • Radar response is affected by soil moisture, irrigation, surface roughness and mixed pixels near field boundaries.
  • Area estimates depend on the classification and the mask. Background/no-data class ID 0 is excluded from crop-area charts.
  • Results apply to this study area and Rabi 2025–26; do not extrapolate directly to other years or regions.
  • This dashboard is a research and learning product, not an official agricultural inventory or a basis for insurance, subsidy or legal decisions.
